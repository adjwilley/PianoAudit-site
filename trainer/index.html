Piano Tuning Trainer

Level 3: Inharmonicity

i

Two strings play together, and the second is slightly out of tune. Press Play, then move the tuning slider until the beating stops. Press Submit when you think it's in tune.

On this level each string has a real piano's overtones. A piano string is stiff, so its overtones run a little sharp, more so the higher they go. Both strings share that stretch, but each overtone beats at its own speed: the second twice as fast as the first, the third three times as fast. Listen for the fast shimmer high in the tone as well as the slow pulse underneath.

The display shows the beats. Bright and dark bands are beats. Far out of tune, the stripe splits in two. One steady stripe means in tune. Switch the display off to train by ear alone; it comes back when you submit.
