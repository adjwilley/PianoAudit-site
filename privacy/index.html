PianoAudit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

PianoAudit is made by Willey Piano Tuning (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the PianoAudit app, on iOS and Android, and the pianoaudit.com website. In short: what you record and measure stays on your device, and anything we collect describes how the software is used.

Microphone

PianoAudit uses your device’s microphone for one purpose: measuring the pitch of piano notes in real time while you run a measurement. Audio is analyzed in your device’s memory as it arrives and is immediately discarded. Audio is never recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere. The app keeps only the numbers derived from the sound: note frequencies, pitch offsets, and inharmonicity values.

Your piano data

  • Piano measurements, tuning curves, and piano names you enter are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage.
  • If you fill in the optional business details for PDF reports (business name, technician name, phone, email, website), those are stored only on your device and appear only in the PDF reports you generate.
  • Reports and data files leave your device only when you explicitly share or export them (for example, emailing a PDF to a customer or AirDropping a backup file). Where they go is entirely under your control.
  • Your device’s standard backup (iCloud on Apple devices, Google backup on Android, or a computer backup) may include the app’s data, like any other app. Those backups are governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy, not ours.

Usage and crash reports

The app does not send us usage or crash data today. A later version may, through a standard service such as Google Firebase, so that we can see which screens people actually use, how often a measurement runs to completion, and what the app was doing when it crashed. That is the information that shows what to fix, and whether the app is being used enough to keep building.

If we add it, it will describe the software and how it behaves. There is no account and no sign-in, so no name or email address is attached to any of it. We do not sell data or run advertising, and none of it will be used to market to you. Your measurements, your piano names, and your audio are not part of it.

This website

The pianoaudit.com website uses Google Analytics to count visits and show which pages people read. It is set up not to store a cookie or anything else in your browser, which is why you are not being asked to accept one. Google receives the page you viewed, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, and basic details about your browser. It cannot recognize you on a later visit, and it never sees anything about your piano.

Purchases

Upgrades (Plus and Pro) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store on iOS, or by Google through Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details. Apple and Google provide us aggregate, anonymized sales information only. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Children

PianoAudit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding the usage reporting described above, the policy will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
